OUR STORY

Good food brings us together.

FoodieHub is a student-built food discovery and ordering platform designed to connect customers with neighbourhood kitchens. This project demonstrates Django MVT, role-based access, transactional checkout and server-side pricing.

FoodieHub is an original project and is not affiliated with any other delivery platform.
